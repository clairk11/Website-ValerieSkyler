Project Overview

Unscripted is a documentary-style short film focused on capturing authentic human moments in natural environments. The goal of the project was to present real stories without staging or scripted dialogue, emphasizing genuine emotion and atmosphere.

Concept

The creative direction centered on natural storytelling. Instead of structured scenes, the filming approach relied on observational cinematography, allowing real interactions and spontaneous moments to shape the narrative.
